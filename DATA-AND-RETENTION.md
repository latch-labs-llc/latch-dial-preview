# Latch Dial — data and retention

Applies to the **optional** Latch service (`worker/`, a Cloudflare Worker + KV).
A build without `VITE_SERVICE_URL` sends nothing to it. Payments themselves are
on Solana, which is public: wallet addresses, amounts and deal accounts can be
read by anyone, whatever this service does.

The app shows the same table in **Settings → Data & privacy → Data & legal
requests**. Both come from `src/lib/retention.ts`; `test/retention.test.ts`
fails if this file, that table, and the KV writes in `worker/src/` disagree, or
if any write has no expiry.

### Messages

Messages are encrypted on the sender's device with a key only the two wallets
can derive (X25519 agreement between keys each wallet publishes in a signed
claim, `mk:`). Each wallet has its own inbox (a Durable Object) holding a copy
of every message it sent or received: the ciphertext, the two wallet addresses,
a thread id, the send time and the sender's device signature; the service cannot
read what was written or what terms were proposed. An open conversation keeps a
WebSocket to that inbox that carries only a one-word "new" nudge. Those keys are static: there is no forward secrecy, so anyone who
later obtains a party's messaging key (a signature from that wallet over the
domain-bound key message) can read that party's whole conversations. Each party
keeps its own decrypted copy on its device; records expire after 90 days.

## Passes through, not stored

<!-- transit:begin (generated by test/retention.test.ts) -->
- When you upload a backup, the payment's deal address travels with it: the service checks on Solana (through its RPC provider) that your wallet is a party, then discards it. Only a scrambled id is stored. The service and that RPC provider see the address while the check runs.
- Your IP address reaches the service with every request; it's kept only as listed above (faucet and rate-limit records).
<!-- transit:end -->

## What it never keeps

<!-- notkept:begin (generated by test/retention.test.ts) -->
- Your name, photo or phone number from Telegram
- Agreement text, amounts or deal terms (backups are encrypted on your device first)
- What your messages say or what proposed terms contain (messages are encrypted on your device first; the service sees only ciphertext and which wallets wrote to each other, when)
- Your contacts, payment history or settings (they stay on your device)
- Any key that can move money
<!-- notkept:end -->

## Deleting

- **Release username**, **Unlink Telegram**: one wallet signature each.
- **Delete backups**: `DELETE /backup/<wallet>` signed by the wallet
  ("Latch backup delete v1"), all or one.
- **Turn off messages**: `DELETE /keys` signed by the wallet ("Latch messaging
  keys remove v1"); nobody can send to the wallet afterwards. **Delete
  conversation**: `DELETE /msg/<wallet>/<thread>` (or all), signed by the wallet
  ("Latch message delete v1"). Removes this wallet's inbox pointers and the
  records it sent (with the other party's pointers to them). Records the other
  party sent stay theirs until they delete them or they expire after 90 days.
- **Delete everything**: Settings → Data & privacy → Clear data → "Also delete
  your data from the Latch service". `DELETE /me`, signed by the wallet
  ("Latch delete my service data v1"). Removes the handle (and any hold in this
  wallet's name), the Telegram link, all backups and the backup writer key,
  the messaging keys and the messages as above.
  `li:` (last signed-request time) stays until it expires 20 minutes later so
  signatures made before the deletion can't be replayed.
- Everything else expires on its own (column "Kept").

Cloudflare's own request logs and analytics are outside this table; the
operator must configure and disclose them separately.

## Legal requests

The operator can produce only what is listed above for the wallet named in a
request. It cannot decrypt backups (keys are derived on the user's device from
a wallet signature), cannot see agreement text, and holds no key that can move,
freeze, refund or redirect money in a deal. On devnet the Latch program is
upgradeable by its maintainer; that is a property of the program, not of this
service. Requests about a deal's funds concern the parties and the public
Solana record.
